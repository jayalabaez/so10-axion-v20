# V44 research stopping rule for the V43 G1--G8 program

Status: `V43_FULL_THEORY_CANDIDATE_TERMINATED__EFT_RESULTS_RETAINED__REOPEN_ONLY_ON_ARCHITECTURE_LEVEL_WITNESS`

## Decision

V43 should be closed as a **complete-theory candidate**.  It should not be
described as a proved no-go for every possible ultraviolet theory.  The
scientifically defensible statement is narrower: four consecutive rebuilds,
V40--V43, have promoted zero full gates while converting the remaining
same-architecture repairs into analytic obstructions.  Continuing to alter
ordinary additive charge tables inside that architecture is therefore no
longer a justified research strategy.

The exact subresults remain useful as conditional EFT and model-building
theorems.  Terminating the complete-theory claim does not invalidate the Z9
operator selection result, the Dirac-messenger matching, the isolated source
branch, the anomaly ledgers, the G7 counterexample, or the quantified G5
Landau-pole exclusion.

## Audited hypothesis class

The stopping decision applies to the V40--V43 architecture: a weakly coupled
four-dimensional polynomial N=1 supersymmetric Pati--Salam construction;
ordinary additive U(1), Z_N, and Z_N^R selectors; the declared visible and
source VEV rings; perturbative spectator thresholds that are fully gapped by
the stated remnant-preserving polynomial masses; ordinary no-GS or equal-level
single-GS anomaly cancellation; the V41 Dirac-neutrino messenger direction;
and the isolated V39 dark-Yukawa mechanism.

It does not exclude PS-breaking thresholds, genuinely nondecoupling light
matter, composite or non-polynomial strong dynamics, a fully quantized
multi-axion/Green--Schwarz construction, a specified five-dimensional inflow
theory with a microscopic gapped boundary, or a different UV regulator.  Any
of those is a new architecture and must be judged from its own complete data,
not inferred from a charge assignment.

## Evidence matrix in prose

**G1 -- microscopic consistency.**  Exhausted within scope: choosing another
all-visible-VEV Z_N^R order while retaining type-I neutrino masses and an
equal-level single-GS cancellation; repairing the F-X-H row with
Theta(+/-9)-massed ordinary pairs; repairing the odd X-gravity row with
even-X fully massive Dirac pairs; and repairing the required +8 Pati--Salam
rows with ordinary PS-unbroken Z66-preserving real or Pfaffian blocks.  These
are analytic divisibility, parity, or modulo-33 obstructions, not failed
parameter guesses.  A local packet can cancel all perturbative triangle rows,
but it destroys the old X-derived residual selector.  G1 therefore has useful
EFT anomaly data but no single global microscopic parent.

**G2 and G3 -- source, vacuum, and symmetry breaking.**  Exhausted within
scope: separating the neutral-linear-term source from the host by adding any
number of additive charge labels.  The charged-spurion escape removes the
renormalizable F-term portals, but its minimal gauged realization has no
zero-FI D-flat nonzero branch and has uncancelled U(1)_S anomalies.  The
obvious neutral compensator restores forbidden host-driver portals.  Thus the
isolated F-flat construction is an EFT source module, not a physical vacuum.

**G4 -- mediation and hierarchy.**  No hidden W, K, gauge-kinetic function,
soft-term derivation, mu/Bmu mechanism, or radiative electroweak-breaking
calculation exists.  This gate is dependency-blocked by G1--G3; performing a
soft-sector fit before choosing a consistent parent would only add free
inputs.

**G5 -- dark and cosmological sector.**  The stated isolated Yukawa point is
excluded: lambda_D=1.563065 has a one-loop pole at 9.54e7 GeV, below
f_PQ=5e11 GeV.  Requiring perturbativity to f_PQ gives lambda_D<=1.166648,
only 0.310 of the target annihilation proxy, and Omega h^2>=0.383 under the
same scaling.  This exhausts that mechanism, not all dark dynamics.  The
U(1)_D vector alternative is only a perturbativity witness and cannot be
promoted without a selector-compatible spectrum and a coupled cosmological
calculation.

**G6 -- running and spectrum.**  The enlarged threshold inventory is known,
but boundary conditions, Abelian kinetic mixing, complete beta functions,
threshold matching, pole masses, and uncertainty propagation are absent.
Because every attempted G1--G3 repair changes that inventory, a full RG run
now would answer a model that has not been defined.

**G7 -- baryon/lepton protection.**  The Z9 theorem protects the conventional
same-orientation Q4/Qc4 ring for the declared VEVs, but a degree-ten six-Qc
witness survives the older selectors.  Z4_M blocks it arithmetically, yet the
necessary ordinary discrete-gravitational screen fails.  More generally, the
required three NDirac fields leave only ordinary no-GS orders 2, 3, and 6,
which do not block the witness; the scan through N=96 is backed by the
analytic charge relation.  Two orientation-neutral bounded-frontier rows and
the full component/Kahler/soft ring remain unclassified.  Another ordinary
charge-order scan is therefore not justified.

**G8 -- flavour and neutrino matching.**  The vectorlike Pati--Salam messenger
is a valid local derivation of W_eff=-(y1 y2/M_F) Q H Sc NDirac and preserves
the audited local selectors and anomalies.  It is worth retaining.  It is not
a flavour theory: no three-family texture, CKM/PMNS fit, soft alignment,
physical spectrum, correlated likelihood, or withheld prediction exists.
This work should wait until G1--G3 and G7 identify the actual light theory.

## Terminal closure criterion

The V43 full-theory line is terminal unless a **single versioned candidate**
simultaneously supplies all of the following, without borrowing mutually
incompatible sectors from different versions:

1. the compact global gauge group, integral charge/axion lattice, complete
   chiral spectrum, and cancellation of every perturbative and global anomaly
   by an explicitly quantized mechanism;
2. a complete renormalizable W, K, and gauge-kinetic sector with an exact
   F- and D-flat symmetry-breaking branch, gauge quotient, full-rank heavy
   mass matrices, and the claimed residual selector;
3. UV-to-EFT matching for the superpotential, Kahler, gauge-kinetic, and soft
   operator rings, including an all-order or Hilbert-basis certificate for the
   dangerous G7 classes; and
4. only after items 1--3 pass, a physical spectrum/RG calculation followed by
   cosmology and a versioned flavour/observable likelihood.

A construction that merely zeros one anomaly row, blocks one operator, or
adds an FI/GS coefficient without its quantized origin does not reopen the
candidate.

## One calculation that could reopen the program

Run one preregistered **joint UV-feasibility calculation** for an anomaly-free,
zero-FI, D-flat charged-spurion completion of the U(1)_F -> Z9 branch, allowing
PS-breaking spectator thresholds rather than the already-excluded
PS-unbroken blocks.  Treat the charge lattice, complete spectator inventory,
renormalizable couplings, F/D equations, heavy mass determinants, residual
group, and dangerous-operator Hilbert basis as one coupled exact problem.

A single exact witness satisfying all of those conditions would reopen G1--G3
and G7 and justify restarting G4--G8.  A certified UNSAT result within declared
representation, multiplicity, charge, and polynomial-degree bounds would
close that entire bounded weakly coupled 4D extension class.  An inconclusive
or unbounded scan is not positive evidence and should not restart charge-table
iteration.

## Preservation label

Archive the line as
`REJECTED_AS_COMPLETE_THEORY__RETAINED_AS_CONDITIONAL_SUSY_PS_EFT_AND_NO_GO_TOOLKIT`.
This is the end of V43 as a full theory, but not a claim that nature admits no
different UV completion.

The stopping rule treats microscopic anomaly data as indispensable because
heavy fermions need not decouple from discrete-anomaly accounting
([Ibanez](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9210211)), the answer depends on the
actual Spin/discrete global structure
([Hsieh](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.02881)), and a proposed inflow or gapped
boundary must itself be supplied and checked rather than named
([Witten--Yonekura](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08775),
[Cordova--Ohmori](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.04962)).
