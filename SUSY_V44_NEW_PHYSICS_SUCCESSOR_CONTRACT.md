# V44 new-physics successor contract

Status: `V44_SEQUESTERED_5D_SPIN10_INTERVAL_SELECTED_AS_SOLE_SUCCESSOR__V43_PORTAL_SCAN_CORRECTED__MICROSCOPIC_INSTANTIATION_NOT_PRESENT__ZERO_GATES_PROMOTED`

## Decision

The only actionable successor among the three audited architecture classes is a
**sequestered five-dimensional Spin(10) interval**.  This is a replacement UV
architecture, not an extension of the excluded four-dimensional
`U(1)_F x U(1)_X x U(1)_H` parent.  In particular, it does not retain the old
`U(1)_X/U(1)_H/Z5610` parent as an exact microscopic factor.

The workspace contains enough exact four-dimensional data to formulate and
falsify this route, but not enough microscopic five-dimensional data to
instantiate it or close a gate.

## Correction to the V43 charged-spurion result

The V43 degree-three portal scan omitted the anomaly-sector singlets from its
candidate set.  The renormalizable monomial

`NDirac E3 Omega`

is a Pati--Salam singlet and has

`(U1F,U1S,Z4R,Z5610,PQ) = (0,0,2,0,0)`.

It is therefore allowed by every declared V43 factor.  This falsifies V43's
claim that the renormalizable source--host portal list is empty.  It does not
by itself remove the formal zero-matter-VEV F-flat branch, because the new term
and its first derivatives vanish at `NDirac=E3=0`; it does change the singlet
mass/flavour problem once `Omega` has a VEV.  Consequently the charged-spurion
packet cannot be used as a protected separation theorem.

The selected 5D successor does not contain `Omega` or `U(1)_S`.  It returns to
the V41 neutral source and obtains separation from five-dimensional locality.

## The selected microscopic skeleton

The candidate spacetime is `M4 x [0,L]`, represented by a supersymmetric
`S1/(Z2 x Z2')` interval.  The provisional bulk gauge algebra is
`so(10) + u(1)_F`; its compact global quotient is an input still to be fixed.
The field partition is unambiguous at the contract stage:

- The **PS/matter wall at `y=0`** preserves
  `(SU(4)c x SU(2)L x SU(2)R)/Z2_diag x U(1)_F`.  It contains
  `Q,Qc,H,X,Zp,P,Pb,PsiBar,Psi,PsiC,PsiCBar,NDirac,A2,A32,A15,A17,A16`
  and the V40 anomaly-cancelling fields
  `L0,Lminus9,R0,Rplus9,E4,E5,E3,E6,Eminus2,Eminus7`.
  It does **not** contain `ThetaPlus`, `ThetaMinus`, `STheta`, `Sc`, `Sbc`,
  `SigC`, or `SigBc`.
- The **source/GUT wall at `y=L`** initially preserves Spin(10) and contains
  only `STheta,ThetaPlus,ThetaMinus` from the V40 source, with
  `W_F=kappa STheta(ThetaPlus ThetaMinus-v_F^2)`, plus a provisional
  `U(1)_F`-neutral `126 + bar126` rank-reducing Higgs sector.  An aligned
  D-flat 126 VEV has SU(5) as stabilizer and can preserve matter parity.  Its
  full superpotential, alignment and mass rank are not present.
- `Sc,Sbc,SigC,SigBc` are deleted rather than moved.  Their old PS-breaking
  function is replaced by the intersection of the PS wall with the SU(5)
  stabilizer on the opposite wall.  The common four-dimensional gauge group
  is intended to be the Standard Model, but this must be proved for the
  actual global quotient and parities; it is not inferred from Lie-algebra
  names alone.
- The starting bulk contains only the Spin(10) and `U(1)_F` vector
  multiplets.  In particular, the old cross-wall Theta-mediated anomalon
  masses and the `Sc`-dependent Dirac messenger are **not** claimed to exist.
  Every needed bulk hypermultiplet or nonlocal mass chain must be added later
  through a full parity, anomaly and KK-determinant manifest.

This layout uses the already certified isolated V41 source branch:
`STheta=0`, `ThetaPlus ThetaMinus=v_F^2`, and at zero FI
`|ThetaPlus|=|ThetaMinus|`.  Thus it is F- and D-flat at the isolated
renormalizable level and leaves `Z9`.  Because `X/Zp` live on the other wall,
the V42 source--host monomials are absent from the **local** 5D action.  Their
nonlocal regeneration is a matching calculation, not an assumed zero.

## Why this one architecture addresses the four linked problems

### Selector and G7

At the PS wall, microscopic local operators contain neither the `U(1)_F`
breaking Theta fields nor any GUT/PS-breaking VEV.  They must respect the full
continuous `U(1)_F`, even though the opposite wall later Higgses it to `Z9`.
Every PS-wall SU(4) fundamental in the displayed chiral packet
(`Q,Psi,PsiCBar`) has `U(1)_F=+3`, and every antifundamental
(`Qc,PsiBar,PsiC`) has `U(1)_F=-3`.  For any local PS invariant their net
orientation is `n_4-n_bar4=4k`, so its `U(1)_F` charge is `12k`.
Local `U(1)_F` neutrality therefore forces `k=0`.  In particular, every
local same-orientation epsilon source with nonzero net orientation is absent
at all orders before cross-wall matching.  This statement does not classify
orientation-zero multi-matter operators or their components; that Hilbert
basis remains a deliverable.

The explicit V42 witness
`ThetaPlus^2 (Qc)^6 (Sbc)^2` is absent as a local operator in this
architecture: `ThetaPlus` is on the source/GUT wall and `Sbc` is deleted.
The other bounded V42 rows involving `Sc/Sbc` are removed for the same reason.
This is not yet a proton-stability proof.  Any generated baryon operator must
be extracted from regulated bulk propagation or other nonlocal physics, and
its coefficient must satisfy a physical decay bound.

### Source and vacuum

Locality distinguishes `STheta` from the PS-wall `X/Zp` drivers without a new
additive charge or the flawed `Omega` construction.  The canonical V41 source
branch supplies the local F/D witness.  The full boundary-Higgs, radion,
Kahler and soft potential remains to be solved.

### Anomalies

The V40 four-dimensional sum has zero perturbative mixed-PS, gravitational
and cubic `U(1)_F` anomalies.  The split source pair has equal and opposite
`U(1)_F` charges and the deleted `Sc/Sbc/SigC/SigBc` fields carry zero
`U(1)_F`; consequently the proposed partition is compatible with that
integrated arithmetic.  This is **not** a localized-anomaly calculation and
does not certify either wall.

Orbifold projections, boundary conditions, gauginos and any future bulk
messengers can generate localized parity anomalies.  Localized anomaly
cancellation is therefore explicitly missing.  A five-dimensional
Chern--Simons or eta-inflow term may be used only after a regulated boundary
calculation shows a globally vanishing distribution and its level is integral
in the actual charge/global-form normalization.  Inflow cannot be postulated
to cancel a surviving four-dimensional anomaly zero mode.  The architecture
does avoid the V38/V39 mirror-family premise and the V42/V43 `U(1)_X`
threshold lattice obstruction because neither the mirror packet nor the old
`U(1)_X` parent is imported.

## Why the other two routes are not selected

- A **non-Abelian finite selector** could in principle alter the invariant
  ring and can have anomalies removed by a symmetry extension.  The workspace
  has no finite group, irreducible-representation assignment, Clebsch data,
  full invariant ring, or Spin-bordism class that simultaneously preserves
  the Yukawa/source terms and forbids all G7 classes.  It presently addresses
  neither source D-flatness nor the driver portal by itself.
- A **composite/topological route** can evade polynomial mass-pairing lemmas;
  supersymmetric confining theories can generate Pfaffian superpotentials and
  an anomalous boundary topological sector can carry inflow.  No confining
  gauge group, preon spectrum, exact moduli space, dynamical superpotential,
  anomaly-matching map, or gapped topological boundary action matching this
  theory exists in the workspace.  It would add the most unknown microscopic
  data while not automatically controlling the source or G7 coefficients.

These are not impossibility theorems.  They are reasons not to run three
unfixed research programs in parallel.

## Missing microscopic deliverables

The 5D successor is instantiated only when one versioned candidate supplies
all of the following in a mutually consistent convention.

1. **Global geometry and group:** interval/orbifold action, compact
   `[Spin(10) x U(1)_F]/Gamma`, boundary gauge groups, all parity matrices,
   Spin/R structure, and a proof that the zero-mode gauge group has exactly
   the intended Standard-Model global form.
2. **Complete localization manifest:** every vector, hyper and boundary
   multiplet; representation, multiplicity, chirality, `U(1)_F` and residual
   charge; boundary assignment; two orbifold parities; and zero-mode index.
3. **Boundary-Higgs theory:** a complete supersymmetric superpotential and
   Kahler/gauge data for the candidate `126+bar126` sector, with F/D equations,
   vacuum alignment, gauge quotient, eaten modes and a full-rank physical mass
   matrix.  A different rank-reducing sector is allowed only if specified at
   the same level.
4. **Localized anomaly and inflow ledger:** regulated anomaly density at both
   walls for gauge, mixed Abelian, gravitational and residual R/matter-parity
   structures; quantized CS/eta coefficients; and global/bordism checks for
   the actual quotient.  The integrated four-dimensional anomaly must vanish.
5. **Local and nonlocal operator matching:** complete wall-local invariant
   rings plus bulk propagator/KK matching for every cross-wall source-host and
   baryon/lepton violating class.  This packet must also construct full-rank
   cross-wall masses for the `L/R/E` anomaly carriers and a replacement
   neutrino/flavour messenger sector.  The output must give determinants and
   Wilson coefficients, not merely declare sequestering.
6. **Compactification and vacuum:** radion stabilization, boundary tensions,
   5D Planck/cutoff/compactification scales, Kähler and soft terms, and a
   tachyon-free global vacuum with controlled higher-dimensional expansion.
7. **Physical reconstruction:** KK and heavy-threshold determinants, gauge
   unification/perturbativity, Higgs and superpartner spectrum, neutrino and
   flavour likelihood, RG evolution, proton/multinucleon rates, and a new dark
   sector/cosmology.  V39's excluded isolated large-Yukawa dark route is not
   revived by changing the geometry.

## Kill criteria

Stop this successor and close it as invalid if any one of the following is
proved for the fully specified candidate:

- no allowed global quotient and parity assignment gives the intended chiral
  zero modes and exactly the Standard-Model gauge group;
- a nonzero integrated gauge or global anomaly remains, or the required
  inflow coefficient is not quantized;
- the boundary-Higgs/source system has no simultaneous F/D-flat physical
  branch or leaves unavoidable charged massless exotics;
- no parity-consistent bulk messenger packet can give full-rank masses to the
  separated `L/R/E` anomaly carriers without generating an unsuppressed
  source-host or baryon/lepton violating operator;
- a local PS-wall operator violates the claimed continuous-`U(1)_F`
  orientation theorem, or a calculated cross-wall coefficient violates the
  proton/multinucleon bound for every perturbative choice of `L` and bulk
  masses;
- the compactification/KK thresholds destroy perturbativity or gauge
  unification below the cutoff;
- the required flavour, neutrino, soft or dark-sector reconstruction has no
  phenomenologically allowed region.

Until those deliverables exist, the correct result is **not the end of every
possible theory**, but it is the end of the V43 four-dimensional architecture
as a claimed completion.  The 5D contract is one sharply testable successor;
it closes no G1--G8 gate today.
