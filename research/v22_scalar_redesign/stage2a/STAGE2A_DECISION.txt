3.5 Why the 45 is not enough (structural reading)
  - The 45 helped by orders of magnitude, but it did not close the gap.  The competitors went from deep (-0.7 to -74

  - The dominant Lb competitor has the 45 switched OFF (|A| <= 0.02).
      - Every sign-definite 45 portal is quadratic in A, and the A-linear eps terms vanish there, so no 45 portal can
        act on this competitor.
      - It can only be lifted by deepening V* through the A sector.  That route is capped by a0 <= 1/2, with three a0
        channels at 0.500, and by the boxes, with O20, O23, O34 and O36_B02 at their box edges.
  - The other dominant class has the 210 switched off (|Phi| 0.06-0.09).
  - Reading (CONJ): the light masses come from cancelling large bare masses against GUT-vev portals.  Any configuration
    that switches a GUT breaker off un-cancels them, and a portal quadratic in that breaker cannot lift it.

      - s* = -6.4e-6 and -2.9e-7;

  - Untested (CONJ):
      - other orientations in formal rounds;
      - other flavour compositions;
      - other vev ratios.
    Imposing the skeptic's light-sector conditions (section 3.4) can only shrink the feasible set.

3.7 [JDG, EST] Is the tree-level ordering stable at one loop?
  The competitor depths (4e-5 to 1.05e-2, in |Phi*|^4 units) are 8-2000 x smaller than the one-loop coefficient scale at
  these points (sum kappa^2/(64 pi^2) = 0.086).  So the tree-level ordering of these nearly degenerate vacua is not

  This does not change the verdict.  The programme's D3 standard is tree level, and changing the standard after a
  failure is the user's decision.  It does bind any future tree-level PASS with margins this small (lesson F17).

6.1 Binding lessons from Stage 2a (continuing STAGE1 F1-F9)
F10 (NUM) The sign-definite 45 portals reduce the competitor depth but do not remove it: from -0.7 to -74 (486-chart
    designs) down to -4e-5 to -1e-2.  Every coupling point produced (9 of 9) still has lower vacua.  The cumulative
    tally of F1 is now 94/94 (Stage 1) plus 491/543 formal and 44/48 skeptic descents ending lower.
F11 (EXACT + NUM) Portals quadratic in a field vanish on competitors in which that field is switched off.  The dominant
    Lb competitor has |A| <= 0.02, and another class has the 210 off.  Any successor whose lifting relies on terms
    quadratic in a GUT breaker must lift each "breaker switched off" class separately.
F12 (NUM) BFB:
    - sampled rays do not converge (~2400 rays; residual negative quartics ~ -1e-3);
    - max-t points without a certificate are unbounded below (12/12 runaways);
    - the sufficient block-norm certificate works at eps = 1e-3; eps >= 0.05 is infeasible.
F13 (NUM/EST) Light-sector necessary conditions:
    - the tree effective quartic must be >= 0 on the ENTIRE light space, not only on the doublet;
    - every light self-cubic and mixed cubic must be tuned.
    Each mixed cubic is an extra tuning.  Lb needs (8,3)(6bar,2)(6,2) at <~ 1e-5 M_U; La needs (8,1)(8,3)(8,3).  The
    placeholder light masses must not hide instabilities along light directions.
F14 (EXACT) Invariants linear in a real X = 0 field (kappaPPA, Phi^3 A, Phi A^3) induce its vev.  Every such field must
    be left free in a vacuum search.
F15 (NUM, machinery) Competitor margins must be gauge invariant (a distance in row values J, not a Euclidean distance).
    The repo backend drops components <= 1e-14, so use the poly backend for small components.
F16 (EST) At the produced points the heavy scalars lie 5-25 x below the heavy vectors in mass, so "everything heavy at
    M_U" overstates D4 margins.  The tree lambda_eff_all can be dialled over [-2.62, >= 0.40], but the one-loop CW shift
    (-0.42 to +0.06) is not small.
F17 [JDG, EST] Competitor depths <~ 1e-2 lie below the one-loop CW scale (0.086).  Any future tree-level PASS with a
    margin below that scale needs a one-loop comparison with its nearest competitors before Stage 2b (section 6.5).

6.3 Family E (B-L broken by a 16_H with X = +1 and centre class z1; M_R from 16bar_H 16bar_H F F / M_Pl)
  - Removes the Delta_R^{++} trap (R4), because Sigma needs no vev at v_R.
  - Keeps the 126bar as the Yukawa carrier, and with it the F2 drivers and the F11 loophole.
  - Not designed.  D1 is genuinely open: the 16_H is a new DP item, and it may bring new fermion couplings.
  - Needs a spinor block in the chart.
  - Landau threshold: alpha_U^-1 >= 26.9 at two loops and >= 29.4 at three loops.
  P ~ 0.1-0.5 % (CONJ).  The D1 DP takes hours and is the natural first kill.  Cost to a Gate-0 verdict ~2-4 days (EST).

6.4 Directions not recommended
  - Re-open L2 (more orientations, compositions or vev ratios).
      - This would override the pre-registered Stage-1 rule.
      - There is no convergence trend (section 3.6), and t is pinned at the floor.
      - The F13 conditions shrink the feasible set further.
      - P(all kills) <~ 0.2 % (EST).
  - Change the D3 standard to a metastable vacuum or a one-loop V_eff global minimum.
      - This changes the criterion after a failure, and the user required the global minimum.
      - At Lb R3, tree metastability fails anyway (section 3.4).
      - It is admissible only as an explicit user decision, never as a rescue chosen by the programme.
  - v20 + 45 + 54 (keeping the 210): the Landau threshold rises to alpha_U^-1 >= 29.8 at two loops, outside every pass
    window (diagnosis R8, EST fail).
  - Relaxing the boxes: no evidence of a rescue.  The Stage-1 D-B extension to box 8 still failed, and here the binding
    constraints include a0 <= 1/2.

6.5 If the user funds a successor (L3', or E with the obvious substitutions): order, gates and kill criteria
  The gates run in order, and a design is killed at the first failure.  Every number carries a label.
  S0  Skeptic review of the Stage-1 claims.  KILL on any upheld D1-D4 failure.
  S1  Census + D1 on the final scalar list.
        - KILL if: K_min < 17 (DP + LP + MILP), any anomaly is nonzero, the kernel changes PQ, or N_DW != 1.
        - Regenerate every reference value with a literal full-sum evaluator (lesson m1).
  S2  Chart + validation.
        - Reductions to the already validated pieces must be bitwise.
        - Invariance must be EXACT.
        - Finite differences must agree to <= 1e-7.
        - The Goldstone count must be exactly 33 + 1 + 1 = 35 at the SM point.
  S3  Tree existence (the amended G0.1).  A clean G3211 -> SM point needs ALL of:
        - exact stationarity, with every field that has a linear invariant left free (F14);
        - 35 zero modes and 0 negative modes;
        - exactly the declared light content, with the doublet as an exact eigenvector of the flavour composition;
        - t >= 1e-3, |a0| <= 1/2, and |quartic| <= 1 (with the normalisation factors stated);
        - a sufficient BFB certificate (F12);
        - tree lambda_eff_all >= 0;
        - NEW (F13): the light-space effective quartic >= 0 on the whole light space;
        - NEW (F13): every light self-cubic <= 0.71 m;
        - NEW (F13): every mixed light cubic below the margin that keeps the light-space quartic >= 0 after the heavier
          light state is integrated out;
        - S and Phi17 chart values compatible with D1 in GeV (F8).
        KILL if there is no clean point at box 1 and box 4 at >= 6 orientations.
  S4  Switched-off phase-1 screen (NEW, cheap and decisive).
        - Competitors: the Stage-2a banks (428 deep + pools + the Lb/La G1-G3 lower points), embedded with the new
          fields at 0 and at their vevs, plus the switched-off classes (each GUT breaker at 0, and all of them at 0).
        - Run the phase-1 SDP with the necessary conditions only.
        - KILL if it is infeasible at solver accuracy (status optimal, not inaccurate, with s* > 0 by more than 10 x the
          tolerance).
  S5  Gate 0 (STAGE1 G0.2-G0.5, unchanged).
        - At least 3 formal rounds at >= 2 orientations.
        - Probes along every light direction in every round.
        - Lower-point thresholds scaled to the physical light masses.
        - Gauge-invariant margins (F15).
        - Confirmation on an independent evaluator.
        - KILL (G0.4) if every round point has a confirmed lower vacuum beyond 1e-9 |V*|.
        - Information item: the minimum competitor margin against the one-loop CW scale (F17).
  Stage 2b, only if S5 passes.  STAGE1 G1-G6, with these amendments:
    G1  KILL if any of the following holds:
          - after one-loop retuning of every tuning, any mass^2 is negative or the Goldstone count is not 35;
          - a retuned coupling leaves its box;
          - a competitor within the F17 margin lies below V* in the one-loop V_eff (Landau gauge, MS-bar) for both
            mu = |Phi| and mu = 0.3 |Phi| (NEW).

    G4  Two-loop running with one-loop matching on the real spectrum, alpha_s(M_Z) = 0.1180 +- 0.0009.

    G5  KILL if a TeV coloured state is excluded by LHC limits, or lives longer than 100 s without a computed BBN-safe

    G6  KILL if K_min < 17 on the final list, dtheta >= 1e-10 at the point's actual <S> and <Phi17> in GeV, the y_s
        window is empty, or N_DW != 1.
  Certification: U2a at best (the K4 gap remains).  A pass is NUM-level.

6.6 Outlook and recommendation (the decision is the user's)
  - Evidence for a NO_GO of the 210-based family with one tuned light doublet (tree-level global-minimum standard; NUM,
    not a theorem):
      - F1 extended by F10-F11;
      - F2 (EXACT);
      - F5 (one-step B-L breaking near M_U is flavour-dead);
      - F6 (Landau leaves no room beyond the 45, and L2 already sits on the knife-edge).
  - Remaining chance that any direction yields a point passing Stage 2: ~0.3-1 % (EST).  Of that, L3' ~0.05-0.3 % and
    E ~0.1-0.5 % (CONJ).
  - Judge's recommendation:
      - Record the L2 Gate-0 kill and the extended F1 evidence.
      - Do not fund open-ended design work in the expectation of a pass.
      - If the user wants the question closed rather than left open, run only the cheap first steps: L3' S0 + S1 and
        E's D1 DP.  They take hours and stay within the 2-process limit.
      - Decide on L3' S2-S5 from their outcome.  L3' goes first if both survive: it is the only candidate with robust
        Landau, and it reuses 35 validated directions.
