=====================================================================================================================
(a) Discard at the first failure, in the order D1 -> D5.  Two skeptics found a failing criterion earlier than the
    designer: D2 before D3 in D-A and in D-C.  The earlier criterion is recorded as the first failure; the later one is
    recorded as an independent failure.
(b) Survival requires that no skeptic kill is upheld.  For each kill I checked how it was reproduced:
      - D-A: independent J-path re-evaluation agreed to <= 1e-11, plus explicit quartic interpolation;
      - D-B: agreement < 1e-12, plus a private-input LP re-run;
      - D-C: independent values-only evaluation, plus two independent Clebsch constructions agreeing to 1e-12.
    No designer result invalidates any kill.
(c) A required test that was never run is UNDECIDED.  It is not a pass, because a pass cannot be hardened out of an
    undecided.  It is not a failure, because no kill is issued without evidence.  An undecided design is not a Stage-1
    pass.  It may enter Stage 2 only behind a gate that runs the missing test first.
(d) Ranking: by (i) probability of passing Stage 2, then (ii) minimality, then (iii) Stage-2 engineering cost.  The
    probabilities are EST.  A tie in (i) within EST uncertainty is broken by (ii) and then (iii).
