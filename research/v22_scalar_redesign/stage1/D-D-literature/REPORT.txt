0. BOTTOM LINE
--------------
Literature survey -> the closest working analogue of our scalar content is Boucenna-Ohlsson-Pernow (1812.10548): the SAME
210 + 126bar + 10_C, broken in ONE step to the SM, unified by two light 210 colour octets.  Adapted literally (L1) it
fails D2: with v_BL = sigma ~ M_U the type-I seesaw cannot be fitted (renormalisable chi2 = 4368, with the X-allowed d=5
term 377; the fit needs v_BL <= 3.5e13).  Rescuing it with type II (L1b, a tuned light Delta_L) fits the proxy but
breaks proton decay / the Higgs quartic (Delta_L must sit below ~5e12 GeV, then tau <= 1.4e34 yr).
The MINIMAL working modification is L2 = v20 + ONE real 45_H (X = 0, Z17-neutral, centre class 0: a DP duplicate of the
210, K_min = 17 unchanged, EXACT) - the Bertolini-Di Luzio-Malinsky idea: SU(2)_R (and SU(4)) broken at M_U by
<45>_(1,1,3) + <45>_(15,1,1) (+ <210>), B-L broken by the 126bar at v_R in the type-I window, BOP octets for
unification.  It removes the diagnosed root cause (one tuned SM doublet instead of two bidoublets; Delta_R^{++} heavy via
the T3R cubic of the 45; no O(1) H-Delta_R portals needed) and passes D1 (EXACT) and D2 (NUM proxy, chi2 < 1e-5 with an
X-allowed d=5 120-like term of coefficient c ~ 0.1; fails at c = 0.03).  D4 passes nominally but is MARGINAL: the 45
puts the two-loop Landau scale at 1.04 M_Pl,red (45 % of the threshold scan pass).  D3 is UNDECIDED: the mechanism has
EXACT ingredients and R2-A's one-bidoublet LP evidence, but no lift test with the 45 was possible (not in the chart).
Fallback L3' (45 + 54 REPLACING the 210; Babu-Khan / BDM-type content): same D1/D2 status, D4 ROBUST (Landau 77-114
M_Pl at two loops, 98-100 % of the scan pass), but D3 even less tested and the v20 machinery is lost.

1. LITERATURE SURVEY (what the viable non-SUSY SO(10) models do, and what it implies here)
=====================================================================================================================
(a) Boucenna-Ohlsson-Pernow, arXiv:1812.10548 (PLB 792 (2019) 251; erratum PLB 797 (2019) 134902, not seen)
    [LIT-abstract/snippet]: minimal non-SUSY SO(10) x U(1)_PQ broken in ONE step to the SM; precise unification from two
    colour-octet scalars of the 210_H, (8,1,0) and (8,3,0), one LHC-accessible; lighter octet <~ 5.9e10 GeV; tau(p) <
    4.5e34 yr; 10_H + 126bar_H Yukawas, "reasonable" fit (R2-C quotes chi2 ~ 21 with 2018 data).  Scalar content =
    exactly our v20 content (210 + 126bar + 10_C) -> the closest literature analogue of family A1.
(b) Bertolini-Di Luzio-Malinsky, arXiv:0912.1796 (vacuum of minimal non-SUSY SO(10), 45 + 16), arXiv:1202.0807
    (45_H + 126bar_H: stable unifying vacua only with QUANTUM corrections, B-L scale up to 1e14 -> renormalisable
    type-I seesaw) [LIT-abstract]; review arXiv:1010.4649 [LIT-snippet]: 45+126/45+16 chains are tachyonic at tree level;
    "the Higgs sector needs to be extended with a 54_H for consistent SO(10) breaking down to the SM".
(c) Jarkovska-Malinsky-Susic, arXiv:2304.14227 [LIT-abstract]: 45 + 126bar + 10_C is in TROUBLE - low GUT scale (fast
    proton decay in minimally tuned versions) and no perturbative SM-like Higgs doublet.  Preda-Senjanovic-Zantedeschi,
    arXiv:2502.21180 [LIT-abstract/snippet] dispute it and ask for independent confirmation (LR scale possibly at the
    LHC, quark-lepton scale ~1e5 GeV); arXiv:2201.02785 / 2410.19408 (45 + 16 + 10_C + d=5 operators): consistency
    needs a weak triplet, a leptoquark and a colour octet below ~10 TeV, tau < ~1e35 yr [LIT-abstract].
(d) Babu-Khan, arXiv:1507.06712 [LIT-abstract]: 54 + 126bar + 10, one Pati-Salam intermediate scale with D-parity.
    Babu-Bajc-Saad, arXiv:1612.04329: real 10 + real 120 + 126bar realistic fit with PS intermediate scale.
(e) Fits: Joshipura-Patel arXiv:1102.5148 (non-SUSY 10+126bar, type-I dominance "excellent", type-II fails),
    Ohlsson-Pernow arXiv:1903.08241 (only NO, type-I or type-I dominated), Bertolini-Schwetz-Malinsky hep-ph/0605006
    (renormalisable neutrino fits point to non-SUSY) [LIT-abstract].  R2-C (in-house, NUM) showed that with NuFIT 6.0 and
    lattice m_d, m_s the renormalisable 10+126bar type-I fit fails (chi2 >= 110), the obstruction being m_s/m_d(M_U).
(f) Gauged-U(1) axion SO(10): Babu-Dutta-Mohapatra arXiv:2410.07323 (PRL 134, 111803) and arXiv:2412.21157 (JHEP 03
    (2026) 084) [LIT-abstract/snippet]: SO(10) x U(1)_a with H(-2), a real 10'(0), Delta(-2), T(+1), S(+12) and A =
    45 or 210 (charge 0); accidental PQ, N_DW = 1, high-quality hybrid KSVZ-DFSZ axion.  Same X-pattern as ours for the
    Yukawa fields; the breaking field (45 OR 210) is X-neutral, i.e. DP-neutral, exactly as here.  Ernst-Ringwald-
    Tamarit arXiv:1801.04906 (SO(10) x U(1)_PQ axion predictions vs breaking chains) [LIT-abstract].
    (Di Luzio-Ringwald-Tamarit arXiv:1807.09769 is SU(5), not SO(10): noted, not used.)
Lessons used for the design (each re-checked below, not taken on trust):
  L-i  every realistic flavour fit with ONE light doublet needs SU(2)_R broken at the doublet-selection scale
       (one-step SM, or SU(2)_R broken at M_U) - matches the diagnosis root cause;
  L-ii direct breaking needs light coloured states for unification (BOP octets); proton decay is then marginal;
  L-iii the renormalisable type-I seesaw with 10+126bar wants v_R <= ~3e13 (R2-C, in-house) while direct breaking puts
       v_BL ~ M_U (the tension that killed minimal SUSY SO(10), hep-ph/0605006);
  L-iv BDM's G3211-type chain (SU(2)_R broken by a 45 at M_U, B-L by 126bar at v_R) keeps the seesaw window, but 45-only
       breaking is tachyonic at tree level and needs either quantum corrections or a 54 (or, here, the 210).
