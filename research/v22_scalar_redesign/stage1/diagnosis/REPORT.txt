0. BOTTOM LINE
One structural fact causes most of the failures. The chain keeps SU(2)_R unbroken down to v_R, so every light Higgs
state is a full bidoublet. On top of that, X forbids the conjugate Yukawa F.F.H10*. Together these force two light
bidoublets, i.e. 10 real tunings of the H-Sigma (1,2,2) mass matrix. That removes the O(M_U^2) mass that would
otherwise lift the H-Sigma plane. No symmetry compatible with the two Yukawas can remove the deep-vacuum drivers
(EXACT, section 1c). The minimal cure is to change the breaking pattern: SU(2)_R and SU(4) should break at M_U, so that
ONE tuned SM doublet can be flavour-capable. New Yukawa fields or charges do not touch the cause.

1. ROOT CAUSES
(a) v20 Pati-Salam witness, flavour-dead. The 210 vev is pure p (a = w = 0) and Sigma = r0 << 1, so SU(4) x SU(2)_R
survives down to v_R.
 - The doublet->Sigma responses of O15/O38/O45 vanish at a = w = 0 (EXACT, G8 structural module (E)).
 - O28 feeds the up type only, so no (15,2,2) admixture reaches the down/lepton sector.
 - The Weyl element S_de gives spec(M_d) = spec(M_e).
 - The light doublet is Re H, so tan(beta) = 1 and CKM = 1.
 - CONJ (K4: no degree-2 SOS at D-odd G3221, 179 flat modes): the residual symmetry that made degree-2 SOS work is the
   one that kills flavour.
(b) G3221 one-bidoublet lemma (EXACT). X(F) = 1 and X(H10) = X(Delta) = -2 are fixed by the Yukawas, so
X(F.F.H10*) = 4. Every scalar that can carry a d=4 Yukawa (10, 126bar, 120) must have X = -2, and its Yukawa is
holomorphic. Hence any single light complex bidoublet, whatever mixture of 10/126bar/120 it is, has
M_u/k_u = M_d/k_d up to Clebsches, i.e. s = 1.
 - A second 10_H or a 120_H does not escape the lemma (EXACT, same argument).
 - The only d=5 conjugate term, F.F.H10*.S*/Lambda, is about 1.2e-5 c. Since M_d - M_u/r ~ Ytilde k_u ~ 2c MeV, it
   reaches V_us and V_cb only for c ~ 10-20 (EST).
(c) Two-bidoublet deep H-Sigma vacua (all 52 R2-A endpoints decomposed per operator, NUM).
 - Class = operators supplying >= 60% of the depth:
   - 42/52 H-Sigma-mixed. Mean normalised contributions: O31 (H+^2 Sigma^2) -0.87|dV|, O28 -0.41, H-singlet portals
     -0.40, Phi^2 H+H (54) -0.30.
   - 7/52 H-mass. m10^2 ~ -16 is cancelled at the vacuum by O33 (lambda33 x^2 ~ 16). Phi210 moves to |Phi| ~ 2.5,
     |H| ~ 2.
   - 2/52 Sigma-self (O27_B02); 1/52 mixed.
 - |c_O31| is only 0.008-0.05. The depth is |H|^2|Sigma|^2 ~ 1e2 times a tiny sign-indefinite holomorphic coupling,
   with the H self-quartics mostly at the +1 box edge.
 Why nothing is left to lift it:
  (i) Two light bidoublets require the whole 4x4 (1,2,2) mass matrix to be O(v_R^2) (10 conditions, EXACT count). The
      bare masses are then fixed by cancellation against the Phi and Phi17 portals, which leaves no positive O(M_U^2)
      mass on the H-Sigma plane.
      Contrast (R2-A, NUM): the same feedback cuts are liftable with one bidoublet (C0, t = 0.033) or none
      (V0, t = 0.031). With two, every cell is infeasible.
  (ii) Symmetry route closed (EXACT). Let Z_n charges be f_i, h, d, q (Phi210 real, so 2q = 0). F_i F_j H and
       F_i F_j Delta on a shared entry give h = d. Then O31 ~ 2(d-h), O28 ~ d-h and O35 are invariant under EVERY
       abelian symmetry that keeps both Yukawas. O15 and O45 survive iff Phi is even.
       Correction to FINAL section 4: making Phi odd forbids O15 and leaves O28 allowed.
       CP does not help: the quartic aligns the field phases, so the depth scales with |lambda31|.
  (iii) The 126bar self-coupling is forced negative.
       - c(O27_B02) sits at the lower box edge -1 in 264/332 R2-A LP solutions (NUM).
       - K3: O27 >= 0 is infeasible even with no tuned light state. The reason is that m^2(Delta_R^{++}) ~ -beta2 r^2
         > 0 requires a negative |Delta.Delta|^2 weight when the 126bar alone breaks B-L at v_R << M_U.
       - |Sigma|^4 invariants are self-conjugate, so no symmetry forbids them (EXACT).
 Root: the 126bar is B-L breaker, Yukawa carrier and H's doublet partner at once; G3221 puts all three at v_R.
(d) lambda_eff and CW.
 - Lifting needs O(1) H-Delta portals (alpha). The light doublet then has an h h Delta_R^0 trilinear, giving a tree
   matching lambda_eff = lambda - alpha^2/(2 rho) that reaches <= -0.089.
 - The heavy-scalar CW shift is ~ -1.3 (kappa_rms 0.67).
 - Separately, every tuned light multiplet with m^2 ~ v_R^2 receives kappa M_U^2/16pi^2 corrections of 1e5-1e7 x tree
   (K3, NUM). This is the non-SUSY hierarchy problem, counted once per tuned multiplet: C0 3, C2 10, C1+(6,2,2) 12.
(e) Flavour m_s/m_d. M_d = H + F and M_e = H - 3F with two 3x3 symmetric matrices. The theta23-compatible basin
predicts m_s/m_d(M_U) = 34.6, against the lattice 19.9 (skeptic, NUM). The fit passes with a 10-20% m_d, m_s floor
(22.6 / 6.9).
 - Default c ~ O(1) d=5 operators (F F H10 Phi210/Lambda, F F Delta Phi210/Lambda ~ 1e-2 c) exceed y_d ~ 5e-6 and
   y_s ~ 1e-4 at M_U. So the renormalisable fit is not a self-consistent truncation (EST); a ~30% shift needs only
   c ~ 1e-4 to 3e-3.
 - In C2 the cause is different: the perturbativity cap M_R <= f v_R ~ 1e11-1e12, against the M_R3 ~ 3-6e12 the fit
   needs.
(f) Unification, proton decay and Landau.
 - This is a three-way squeeze in G3221: v_R >= 2e12 (seesaw), M_U high enough for tau, and a weak enough alpha_U.
 - Light coloured states that raise v_R either lower M_U (C1: 2.2e15) or strengthen alpha_U. C1+(6,2,2) has
   alpha_U^-1 = 25.7, which gives Lambda_3L = 0.48.
 - The SO(10) phase is heavy with B63 + 210 + 126bar: b = (5.33, 3482, 1.6e5) (my screen reproduces R2-B's
   Lambda_2L/3L to 3%).
 - Landau needs alpha_U^-1(M_U) >= 26.3 (2L) / 28.9 (3L) at M_U = 1e16, and 27.6 / 30.2 at 6e15 (NUM, +-1).
 - Proton (lattice x A_R normalisation, from C2's numbers): M_U >= 6.6e15 x sqrt(34.6/alpha_U^-1) GeV (EST +-25% in
   tau).

   (15,2,2) admixture. Under X this needs SU(2)_R AND SU(4) breaking in the H-Sigma mixing at the selection scale:
   (a != 0 or a (15,1,1)) and (w != 0, a 45 (1,1,3) vev, or v_BL ~ M_U). [(a), (b)]
