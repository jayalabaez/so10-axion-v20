11. What decides the gate, and the verdict
-----------------------------------------
Two loops (gauge + Yukawa, one-loop thresholds): unification exists for T2 and T3 everywhere in the window, Lambda_2L =
1.03-2.4 M_Pl,red at nominal thresholds (1.03 only for M_s = 3e12 with the Phi pair at M_U; 1.3-2.4 for Phi >= 1e17);
the Planck operator never breaks it (min 2.0 (T2) / 1.8 (T3) M_Pl,red over the whole c-scan at M_s = 1e13); the
combined threshold Monte Carlo keeps it above M_Pl,red in [P2_1E13]% of the samples at M_s = 1e13, 100% at 7e12 and
5e12 (Phi at 1e17; min 1.39 / 1.16 M_Pl), 91-97% at 3e12 (Phi at 1e17; min 0.91-0.96) and only 53-56% at 3e12 with
Phi at M_U (min 0.57-0.62).  So the two-loop criterion is PASSED robustly for M_s >= 5e12 (all c, all thresholds,
Phi pair >= 1e17) and only marginally at the bottom of the window.
Three loops (gauge + matter b3; Yukawa two-loop terms included; three-loop Yukawa/quartic terms not available):
nominal Lambda_3L/M_Pl,red = 1.05 (T2 and T3) at M_s = 1e13, Phi at 1e17; 1.08 with the Phi pair at the unitarity
limit 1.25e17; < 1 for M_s <= 7e12 unless y_phi >= 3.5 (a0 = 2, excluded by the a0 <= 1/2 normalisation).  The sign of
Lambda_3L - M_Pl,red is set by:
  (i)  the Planck gauge-kinetic coefficient: with physical solutions (v_R in [8e12, 1e14], M_U <= 1e17) the
       three-loop test passes for EVERY 210 orientation (th, s) only for c in [-0.3, +0.1] (T2) and [-0.2, 0.0] (T3);
       c = +0.2 (T2) / +0.1 (T3) already fails for some orientations.  Sensitivities at c = 0 over the orientations:
       d ln Lambda_3L/dc in [-0.44, +0.05] (T2), [-0.65, +0.09] (T3); d ln v_R/dc in [-1.2, +0.8] / [-1.5, +1.0];
       d ln M_U/dc in [-1.8, +0.9] / [-2.7, +1.4]; d alpha_U^-1/dc in [-2.3, +3.6] / [-3.6, +5.6]; per unit c the
       largest |Delta alpha_i^-1| is 0.7-1.2 (T2) / 0.9-1.6 (T3) units (k1_planck_smallc.json).  So c = 0.1 moves
       Lambda_3L by up to -4.4% (T2) / -6.5% (T3), v_R by x1.1-1.2 and M_U by up to x1.3: comparable to or larger than
       the +5% margin.  Beyond |c| ~ 0.3-0.5 the continuation either leaves the physical region (M_U up to 6e19,
       v_R ~ 1e10-1e16) or finds no perturbative solution.  c is a UV input, not computable here; |c| ~ 1 is the
       naive expectation (LIT-abstract 0805.0145, 1006.1252), so the three-loop result is undecidable without an
       assumption |c| <~ 0.1.
  (ii) the GUT-scale spectrum (x1/2..x2): three-loop pass fraction [P3_GUT]% (median Lambda_3L/M_Pl = [MED3_GUT]);
       the vacuum is not known (G3 BLOCKED), so the spectrum cannot be computed; in the unitarity-favoured half
       (x1/2..x1) the pass fraction is [P3_GUTU]%.
  (iii) the light thresholds (x1/3..x3): pass fraction [P3_LIGHT]%; combined with (ii): [P3_COMB]% at M_s = 1e13,
       29% / 22% (T2 / T3) at M_s = 7e12, 7% / 3% at 5e12, 0-1% at 3e12 (Phi at 1e17; max 1.25 M_Pl).
  (iv) the uncomputed four-loop gauge term: the three-loop term is 0.3 of the two-loop term at M_Pl,red and lowers
       Lambda by a factor 2.3, so a four-loop term of +-30% of the three-loop one (geometric estimate) moves Lambda by
       [B3_075]..[B3_125] (the b3 x0.75 / x1.25 proxy): larger than the +5% margin.
  (v)  the two-loop GUT threshold corrections (not computed): differential <= 0.05 units, coherent <= 0.3 units of
       alpha_i^-1 for a x2 spectrum -> <= 1-7% on Lambda (EST, k1_twoloop_threshold_est.json).
  (vi) three-loop Yukawa terms (EST < 0.5% of b3) and three-loop quartic terms (EST a few % of b3 under a0 <= 1/2,
       the critic's estimate was up to 20% for O(1) quartics): below (iv).
  (vii) U(1)_X feedback at its own perturbativity bound: [U1X] on Lambda_3L; product-group pure three-loop terms in
       the SM/G3221 phases: [SM3L] on Lambda_3L and [SM3L_MU] on M_U (EST, cross terms omitted).
VERDICT: UNDECIDED.  Not FAIL: the window has two-loop passes with margins up to x2.4 and the three-loop test passes for
a non-empty c-range (c in [-0.3, 0.1]) at the top of the window.  Not PASS: the three-loop pass is +5% at nominal
thresholds and flips sign under any one of (i)-(iv), each of which is an uncomputed or unknowable term whose size
exceeds the margin; a three-loop pass requires simultaneously M_s >= ~9e12 (y_s >= 0.31-0.35), the Phi pair at
>= 1e17, c in [-0.3, 0.1] and a favourable GUT spectrum.
Named missing computations that would decide it: (1) the GUT-scale spectrum from an actual vacuum (K3/K4), (2) the
four-loop SO(10) gauge term or, equivalently, a declared convention that the Landau criterion is evaluated at two
loops (then PASS for M_s >= 7e12, all c, >= 90% of thresholds), (3) the two-loop threshold matching at M_U, (4) an
assumption on |c| (<= 0.1 needed), (5) the three-loop quartic terms with the actual quartics.
