2. LEMMA B - rigorous lower bound on K (EXACT; k0b_engine.Model.dual_bound)
q(O) = (G2(O), f_1(O), ..., f_n(O)) = charges of O under the accidental U(1)s (kernel of all Yukawas and the four
phase-sensitive scalar couplings kappa H H S, Phi210 H* Delta, O38, Delta^2 H*^2).  In a closed vacuum diagram vertices
and propagators are neutral, so sum_i q(O_i) = q(vev) = (+-4, 0, ..., 0).  If mu . q(O) <= d(O) - 4 for every singlet
X-neutral operator with 5 <= d(O) <= 20, then every diagram with K <= 16 (all its operators have d <= 20) obeys
K >= 4 mu_0, so mu_0 >= 17/4 proves K >= 17 (the G2 = -4 diagrams are covered by -mu).  mu is found by a cutting-plane
LP; the separation oracle is an integer program (HiGHS, zero MIP gap) over ALL monomials of the fields and their
conjugates with 5 <= dim <= 20 obeying the necessary conditions X = 0, centre z = 0 mod 4, Lorentz parity (even/even,
or odd/odd with one derivative, +1).  Using necessary conditions only can weaken, never invalidate, the bound.  The LP
is bounded by the scalar operator (mu_0 <= 17/4).  Termination: oracle finds no violated monomial (certificate), or
the LP optimum drops below 17 (cannot be certified; K0's explicit closure program is then run).  Subsumes K0's mod-4
lemma (a Z4 character of the same kernel) and the manuscript's P counting.  Test: on F17 the LP drops below 17 after
10 cuts and K0's closures (K = 9 all / 12 variant E, NDA 10^1.1) are reproduced by the rebuilt program.
Monotonicity (EXACT): adding fields adds operators and vertices and can only shrink the kernel, so the true K_min
and the LP bound of a content are <= those of any sub-content containing F and the scalars.  A charge, or a pair of
charges, whose sub-theory bound is < 17 therefore never occurs in a passing candidate: this is the pruning of the
scans (single-multiplet map, two-multiplet maps); every surviving candidate is certified on its complete content.
Remark on K = 17 closures: with the vev monomial Phi17^4 S*^17 (dimension 21) the loop-integral dimension is
D = K - 17, so a K = 17 closure is UV-dominated (M_Pl^-17 like the tree operator, times loop factors and couplings);
extra neutral vev factors (Delta Delta*, kappa lock) only bring (v_R/M_s)^2 ~ O(10) factors.  Hence K_min >= 17 <=>
the fermion-assisted diagrams do not exceed the declared tree estimate (EXACT structure, NUM/EST magnitude).
