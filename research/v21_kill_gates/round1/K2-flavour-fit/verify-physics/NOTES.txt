1. T2 structural obstruction (EXACT, EFT theorem):
   Above v_R the T2 content has ONE light complex bidoublet Phi_L = alpha Phi_10 + beta Phi_126 (the orthogonal
   combination sits at M_U).  X forbids the tilde couplings (F.F.H10* has X = 4), so the quark Yukawa term is
   Q_L Y_L Phi_L Q_R with a single matrix Y_L = alpha* Y10 + beta* Y126 that multiplies BOTH <H_u^L> and <H_d^L>
   (they are components of the same multiplet Phi_L; the S.H10.H10 term mixes H_u^10 with (H_d^10)^c and sets v_d/v_u,
   but cannot change the matrix).  Hence Yu = (v_u/v_d) Yd exactly at tree level, up to the heavy-bidoublet admixture
   O(v_R^2/M_U^2) ~ 1e-6 and the F.16(-3).H10* mixing O(v^2/M^2).  Consequences: V_CKM = 1, m_u:m_c:m_t = m_d:m_s:m_b.
   Numerical lower bound on the charged-sector chi2 (my targets at M_U, 1% floor): s12 pull -331, s23 -52, s13 -35,
   up-mass pulls (+124, +60, -70) at the best common r = 34.8  ->  chi2 >= 1.4e5.  Same conclusion as the sibling
   verifier (s = 1 forced: proxy chi2 3.3e9).  The researcher's free-composition fit is therefore the T3 fit, and the
   statement "T3 is not required" is wrong: a free (c_u10, c_u126, c_d10, c_d126) needs two light bidoublets.
   The researcher's G3221 RGE with four independent matrices Yu, Yd, Ye, Yn above v_R is inconsistent with either
   content (one or two bidoublet Yukawas run there); numerically a ~1% effect over ln(M_U/v_R) = 6.7.

2. RGE convention bug in rge.py/fit.py: the Yukawas are built and diagonalised in the (f_L)_i (f^c)_j convention
   (left rotation from Y Y^dag, Yd = V D) but the beta functions are the LWX/Antusch form Y (Y'^dag Y') whose cross
   terms must act on the doublet index, i.e. the (f^c, f_L) convention.  Test (res/ckm_run_test.py): with the
   researcher's feed s23 = 0.04182 at every scale up to 2e16; with transposed feed s23 -> 0.04724 at 2e16 (+13%),
   the known SM behaviour (top-Yukawa term -3/2 Yu^dag Yu on the Q_L leg; opposite sign to the MSSM).  Their
   GUT-scale CKM targets and the top-down CKM at M_Z are therefore wrong by ~11-13% in s23 and s13; the kappa
   RGE cross term is likewise on the wrong index (negligible for hierarchical NO).  My code calls rge.py with
   transposed Yukawas only.

   seesaw scale (1) = 19 real parameters for 18 observables (type I, free composition); dof = -1, not +1.

4. Composition normalisation (found by the sibling verifier, confirmed): fit.py uses c_d10 = sqrt(1 - c_d126^2(1+|rs|^2))

5. G3221 b-coefficients: the (8,2,2) scalar is counted with 8 SU(2)_L and 8 SU(2)_R doublets (index 4) but with 4
   colour octets (index 12), i.e. as complex for SU(3) and 'half' for SU(2).  A complex (8,2,2) has 16 doublets of
   each SU(2) (index 8): b_L, b_R = 15.0, 9.67 (T2) instead of 13.67, 8.33; a real one would halve the SU(3) count
   instead.  Effect on K2: < 1% in Yukawa running (alpha^-1(M_U) 26.6 vs 28.0 for SU(2)).
