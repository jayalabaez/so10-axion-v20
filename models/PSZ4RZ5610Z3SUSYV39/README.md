# PSZ4RZ5610Z3SUSYV39 source boundary

V39 is a narrow G7 local-source block on top of V37. It replaces the one
Pati--Salam `Sig6` with two chiral sixes:

`Sc^2 SigC + Sbc^2 SigBc`, with `Z3(SigC,SigBc)=(2,1)`.

The new selector has `Z3(Q,Qc)=(1,2)`. It keeps the V37 `Z5610`, external
`Z4R`, displayed Yukawa/vectorlike-mixing terms, and the type-I seesaw source.
Consequently, the local superpotential source monomials `X Q^4`, `X Qc^4`,
`Zp Q^4`, and `Zp Qc^4` have nonzero `Z3` charge and are forbidden.

This does not survive the full PS-VEV-dressed operator ring.  The degree-nine
operators `X [epsilon_SU2R delta_SU4 (Qc Sbc)]^4/M^6` and the corresponding
`Zp` operator are neutral under `Z3` and `Z5610`, have superpotential `Z4R`
charge two, and are PS singlets.  Four canonical `Sbc` VEV insertions are
generically nonzero because each VEV contracts with a dynamical `Qc`.  This is
an explicit algebraic counterexample to full `Qc^4` protection, although its
selected component is not by itself a proton-decay amplitude.

For the retained term architecture, ordinary non-R selector arithmetic gives
`q(P)=0` and `3q(Q)=0`; a nontrivial solution that forbids the four sources is
therefore minimally `Z3`. The chosen anomalon charges also make the standard
mixed Pati--Salam--`Z3` residues and the two raw `Z3`--`Z5610` cross residues
zero modulo 3. That necessary arithmetic is not a full product-bordism or
discrete-R anomaly proof.

The equal-conjugate Pati--Salam branch retains `Sc*Sbc=vPS^2`,
`P*Pb=fPQ^2`, and `X=Zp=SigC=SigBc=0` in the canonical global-SUSY
truncation. The same V37 `Z5610`/PQ assignments are retained, so adding the
two PQ-neutral sixes does not by itself prove inheritance: their nontrivial
`Z3` and `Z4R` charges must be included.  The executable V39 lattice search
does so afresh and finds no PQ-breaking W term below degree 33 and no Kähler
term below degree 32.  Gauge-singlet witnesses `P^33` and
`P^6 A32^21 A16dag (A17dag)^4` attain those bounds.

This source does not establish an all-order PS-VEV-dressed proton-operator
ring, a full finite product anomaly/bordism completion, a component pole
spectrum, a soft vacuum, or a flavour likelihood. It is an executable,
source-level G7 diagnostic candidate, not a complete theory.
