## Terminal verdict

V43 is **not a complete theory and not one integrated EFT**.  It is a
portfolio of alternative, scoped constructions and obstruction certificates.
The defensible classification is:

- **Complete theory:** rejected by its own acceptance contract (`0/8` full
  gates).  This is a deductive completeness failure, not an empirical
  falsification by data because no joint predictive likelihood was produced.
- **Single internally consistent V43 model:** not established.  The spurion,
  old-tadpole `Z4_M`, and alternative threshold branches are not assembled into
  one common action, charge lattice, vacuum, or spectrum.
- **Restricted EFT results:** several remain valid in their stated domains,
  especially the V41 Dirac-messenger matching, the V43 self-Majorana
  gravity-row witness, and the scoped `33 Z` Pati--Salam threshold obstruction.
- **Minimal charged-spurion gauge theory:** excluded as written by its
  nonzero `U(1)_S` anomaly rows and absence of a nonzero zero-FI D-flat branch.
- **Ordinary no-GS `Z4_M` gauge completion:** excluded in the audited universal
  charge/fully-decoupled class; the charge table by itself remains only a
  global-EFT selection rule.

The correct action is to **freeze V40--V43 as a negative research record and
close it as a candidate complete theory**.  Further work is scientifically
meaningful only as a new, single-architecture theory that supplies one common
action and global gauge form before restarting G4--G8.

## V43 portal-scan correction

The V43 charged-spurion scan used
`HOST_CORE_FIELDS + SOURCE_FIELDS`.  `HOST_CORE_FIELDS` is the V40
`VISIBLE_FIELDS` tuple and omits the anomaly-cancelling fields.  Extending the
same degree-three charge scan to the already declared full field table gives
the Pati--Salam-singlet operator

`W ⊃ y_i NDirac_i E3 Omega`.

| Field | `U(1)_F` | `U(1)_S` | `Z4R` | `Z5610` | `PQ/170` | PS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `NDirac` | -3 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | singlet |
| `E3` | +3 | +1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | singlet |
| `Omega` | 0 | -1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | singlet |
| **sum** | **0** | **0** | **2** | **0** | **0** | singlet |

It is therefore a genuine allowed renormalizable source--host--anomalon
portal.  The following V43 statements must be withdrawn or narrowed:

1. “no potentially PS-invariant source-host portal through degree three” is
   true only in the explicitly truncated visible-plus-source field list, not
   in the declared full V43 field content;
2. `renormalizable_source_host_separation_is_symmetry_protected = true` is
   false for the full field table;
3. “every F-flat host solution” may be combined with the source branch is too
   broad.  A restricted target branch with
   `<NDirac>=<E3>=0` can still remain F-flat, but the generic superpotential
   mixes the neutrino and anomalon singlet sectors after `<Omega> != 0`.

Two narrower V43 conclusions survive this correction: no direct
renormalizable `X/Zp`--source portal was found, and the displayed target
zero-VEV F-term branch is not disproved by this operator.
